# 7. Release Notes

## Novel Fair — First Beta Release (v1.0.0-beta.1)

Thank you for trying NovelFair! This is the first public beta release. Here is what you can do today:

**Explore**
- Home feed with featured novels, a rotating banner, popular writers and categories.
- Discover and category screens to browse the catalogue, and full-text search over titles, authors and categories.
- Rich novel detail pages with cover art, author information, and chapter lists.

**Read**
- A customizable reader with two modes — continuous scroll and page (with an optional book-flip effect).
- Adjustable font, font size, line spacing, content width, and multiple reading themes.
- Table of contents, bookmarks, and automatic saving of your reading position — including across devices when you're signed in and sync is enabled.
- Previously opened chapters are cached on your device, so you can keep reading while offline.
- Adult-content titles are clearly labeled and gated behind a confirmation.

**Your library**
- Build a library with reading statuses (Reading, Plan to read, Completed).
- Continue-reading shelf, reading history, bookmarks, favorites and reading statistics, plus achievements and daily reading streaks.

**Account & settings**
- Sign up with email or Google, with email verification.
- Extensive settings: appearance and themes, fonts, reader preferences, animations, storage/cache control, data-sync controls, and privacy options.
- In-app update mechanism to receive new versions.

**Important notes for a beta**
- This is a beta: you may encounter bugs, and features may change or disappear without notice.
- Some settings screens include items that are not yet fully functional (see below).
- Your reading data is synchronized to our servers when sync is on; you can turn sync off in **Settings → Data Sync**.
- If something goes wrong, please report it. Crash reports are only sent after asking for your permission, and you can choose exactly what to include.

**Known limitations in this beta**
- Notification preferences and some privacy toggles are placeholders and do not yet change behavior.
- "Clear Downloads," "Rate on Google Play," "Export my data," and "Open Source Licenses" are placeholders.
- Comments, reviews, payments/subscriptions and user publishing are not available.

We'd love your feedback. Tell us about bugs or ideas at **darkscriptstudio@gmail.com** . Thank you for reading with NovelFair!
