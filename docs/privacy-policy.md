## NovelFair Privacy Policy

*Version 1.0 — Effective date: 16 AUG 2026*

### 1. Introduction

Welcome to NovelFair ("the App"), a mobile application for reading novels. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, why it collects it, how it is stored, and the choices you have.

By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy. Please read it together with our Terms of Service.

### 2. Who we are and how to contact us

The App is operated by **Dark Script Studio** ("we", "us"). For privacy questions, contact us at **darkscriptstudio@gmail.com** .

### 3. Scope

This policy applies to the NovelFair Android application (version `1.0.0-beta.1`). It does not cover websites or services linked to from the App, which have their own privacy practices.

### 4. What we collect

#### 4.1 Information you provide

- **Account information** — if you create an account you provide your email address, display name, and a password. Your password is sent only to our authentication provider (Firebase) and is never stored on your device or in our database by us.
- **Profile information** — you may choose to add your bio, gender, date of birth, and a profile photo.
- **Search terms** — the words you type into search. These are stored only on your device so we can show recent searches.

#### 4.2 Information collected automatically

- **Device and app information** — such as device model, Android version, screen size, language, timezone, app version, and network type. This is included in crash reports if you choose to send them.
- **Crash information** — when the App crashes, we may collect technical diagnostics such as the error details, memory information, and what screen you were on. You are asked for your consent before any crash report is sent, and you can choose which categories of data to include. You are also given the option to include account information (such as your email address); this option is off by default.
- **Analytics** — the App links the standard Firebase Analytics SDK, which by default collects basic usage statistics (for example, when the App is opened) and device identifiers, and processes them on Google's infrastructure. The App does not send custom analytics events. [Note: the in-app "Analytics" toggle currently does not disable this collection.]

#### 4.3 Information generated by your reading

- **Reading progress** — where you are in each book (chapter, page/position, percentage read, reading time, words read, last read time).
- **Reading statistics** — such as books read, chapters read, reading sessions and minutes, and reading streaks.
- **Reading history** — the list of books you have recently read.
- **Bookmarks** — the chapters and positions you have bookmarked.
- **Favorites and library** — books you have added to favorites or your library, and their status (e.g. reading, plan to read, completed).
- **Books you view** — a record that you opened a particular book is sent to our content provider so view counts can be shown.

#### 4.4 Data that stays on your device only

- Detailed per-chapter reading records (reading time per chapter).
- Reader preferences such as font size and reader theme for each book.
- Recent search terms.
- Cached novel content for offline reading.
- Local copies of your account and reading data, stored in an encrypted database on your device.

### 5. How we use your information

- To create and manage your account and let you sign in (email/password or Google).
- To save and restore your reading progress across devices.
- To show your library, bookmarks, favorites, history, reading statistics and achievements.
- To send you notifications about new chapters and updates (see section 8).
- To diagnose crashes and improve the App's stability and performance.
- To deliver app updates (see section 10).

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your data to show behavioral advertising.

### 6. Where your information is stored

- **On your device** — an encrypted local database (Room/SQLCipher) and app storage, protected by your device's security key store. App data is not backed up to your Google account by the operating system (`allowBackup` is disabled).
- **With our service providers** — user account and reading data is stored on Google's cloud (Firebase Firestore and Firebase Authentication). Content and your profile photo are stored with Appwrite (a third-party cloud provider). These providers process data under their own terms. No user reading data is stored in the Appwrite content database.

### 7. Data synchronization

If you are signed in, your reading data (progress, statistics, library, bookmarks, favorites) is synchronized with our servers **by default** so your reading continues where you left off on another device. Synchronization happens in the background periodically (every 6 hours by default) and when you read or change data.

You can turn synchronization off or configure it per category in **Settings → Data sync**. Turning it off stops new uploads but does not delete data already uploaded.

### 8. Notifications

With your permission (on supported Android versions), the App receives push notifications about new content and updates. To deliver these, the App stores a notification token linked to your account and subscribes your device to a general notification channel. You can disable notifications at the operating-system level, or by turning off notification permission in your device settings. [The in-app notification toggles currently do not change what notifications are received.]

### 9. Data security

- All communication between the App and its servers uses encrypted connections (HTTPS). Unencrypted traffic is disabled.
- Your local database is encrypted with a key protected by your device's security hardware.
- Your password is handled by Firebase Authentication and is never stored by the App.
- Access to your personal documents in our database is restricted to your account and to administrators.
- Crash reports are readable only by administrators.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

### 10. App updates

The App may check for newer versions and offer to download and install updates directly. Downloading and installing an update is performed only after you confirm, and update files are verified (checksum) before installation. The update check itself does not send your personal information.

### 11. Data retention

We retain your account, profile and reading data for as long as your account exists. If you delete your account, we remove your user and profile records and your login credentials, but **please note that some reading data records and your profile photo may remain** in our systems until manually cleaned up. (See section 12.)

### 12. Your choices and rights

- **Account deletion** — in the App: **Profile → Settings → Account Center → Delete account**. This signs you out and removes your user/profile records and login. Because of the way deletion is currently implemented, some of your reading data on our servers and your profile photo on our content provider, as well as data stored locally on your device, may not be removed by this action. We are working to improve this. [See "Critical fixes".]
- **Sign out** — in the App: **Settings → Sign out**. Signing out does not delete your data.
- **Clear search history** — on the search screen.
- **Clear cache and limit storage** — in **Settings → Storage**.
- **Delete bookmarks / library books / favorites** — individually from the corresponding screens.
- **Data export** — not currently available in the App. 
- Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights, such as access, correction, deletion, and the right to object or restrict processing. To exercise these rights, contact us at **darkscriptstudio@gmail.com** .
